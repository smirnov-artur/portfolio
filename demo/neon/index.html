Where the number comes from

Measured on the curve

The centre line of every letter is sampled and its true arc length is summed. A wide round W costs more glass than a condensed one at the same height, and the total shows it to the centimetre.

A bend is a heat

Walking the finished tube, turning angle is banked and a bend is booked every 60 degrees, plus one wherever the tube reverses direction. That is what a bender actually charges for.

Glass has a minimum radius

8 mm tube will not take a corner tighter than 17 mm, 12 mm will not go below 27 mm. Choose the thicker bore and the corners of the letters visibly round off — because they must.

Power follows length

Load is metres times watts per metre for that bore; the transformer is then taken from the ladder with 25 per cent headroom. Change one letter and the unit can step up a size.

Notes for a platform partner

No build step, no external hosts

One folder of static files. It runs from a CDN, from a theme directory, or straight off a disk — every asset, font included, is inside the folder. Nothing is fetched at runtime.

The configuration is the URL

Text, lettering, glow, bore, height, backing and mounting live in the query string, so support, the shop floor and the buyer all look at the same sign.

Priced from geometry, not from a table

The same approach carries over to anything cut, bent, welded or printed to order: measure the thing that was configured, then bill the operations it needs.

Written for an integration

Rates, the alphabet and the option lists sit in two plain files at the top of the source. Swapping them for a shop's own catalogue is an afternoon, not a rewrite.
